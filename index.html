Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect your information.

Updated October 17, 2026 Version 1.0

1. Overview

AQFort ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the AQFort mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using AQFort, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We keep this policy clear and straightforward. If you have any questions, please reach out using the contact details in Section 8.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Information you provide: When you contact us or use certain features, we may collect your email address, feedback, or any content you voluntarily submit (e.g., support requests).
  • Usage data (automatic): We may collect anonymized technical data such as app version, device type, operating system, crash logs, and basic interaction statistics. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • No account required: AQFort does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, phone number, or precise location unless you explicitly provide it.

We do not collect sensitive categories (e.g., health, race, religion, sexual orientation) or financial information.

3. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the functionality of AQFort.
  • To respond to your support requests and communicate with you (only if you contact us).
  • To analyze aggregated usage trends to enhance user experience and app stability.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security threats.

We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purposes outside the scope of the Service.

4. Legal Basis & Data Retention

We rely on our legitimate interest in providing a secure, functional app experience. Where you contact us, we process your email address based on your consent and our need to respond.

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Support-related emails are deleted after 12 months. Aggregated usage data is kept for a maximum of 24 months and then permanently deleted.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We may use trusted third-party hosting or crash-reporting services (e.g., Apple App Store / Google Play analytics) that process data under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, regulation, or legal process, we may disclose information when necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

We never share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to any personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we continuously review our security practices to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

AQFort is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.
